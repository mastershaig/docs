Templates are ready-made, editable tests with carefully worded steps, so you can
start from a working test instead of a blank page. Open **Templates** from the
sidebar to browse the gallery.

## The e-commerce gallery

Each template is a complete test you can preview, add to a suite, and edit. The
curated templates are:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Homepage loads and key navigation works">
    Confirms the storefront loads and the primary navigation links go where they
    should.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search returns relevant products">
    Checks that searching for a common product term returns relevant results.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collection filter and sort">
    Opens a collection, applies a filter and a sort order, and verifies the listing
    updates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Product page: variant selection and add to cart">
    Selects a required product variant and adds the item to the cart.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cart quantity change and remove">
    Changes the quantity of a cart line and then removes it, verifying totals update.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Price consistency across listing, product page and cart">
    Captures a product's price on the listing and verifies it stays the same on the
    product page and in the cart.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Coupon / discount code">
    Applies a discount code in the cart and verifies the total reflects the discount.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Checkout up to the payment step (never pay)">
    Walks checkout through contact and shipping up to the payment step, and stops
    before paying.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mobile menu and add to cart">
    On a phone-sized viewport, opens the mobile menu and completes an add-to-cart.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Broken links / 404 sweep of key pages">
    Visits key pages and checks for obvious errors or broken links.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Newsletter / contact form validation (no submit)">
    Checks that the newsletter or contact form validates input, without submitting
    it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Store in each language">
    Runs the same journey once per language via a scenario table, checking the page
    language, currency, and untranslated text.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  The **Store in each language** template comes with a built-in scenario table
  (English, German, French). See
  [Language & locale testing](/language-locale-testing) and
  [Scenario Tables](/scenario-tables).
</Note>

## Starter suite from a store URL

Rather than add templates one at a time, use **Generate a starter suite for my
store**: enter your store URL, pick the templates you want, and Testavi creates a
suite with those tests pointed at your store.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your store URL">
    For example `https://mystore.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick templates">
    Choose the templates to include and name the suite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and run">
    The generated tests use your store URL as their start URL. Open each one and
    tweak the wording for your store's exact labels before running — see
    [Prompting 101](/prompting-101) and
    [the e-commerce testing guide](/ecommerce-testing-guide).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Templates are starting points. Quote your store's exact button and label text in
  the steps to make the tests more reliable.
</Tip>
